Reacgen Biosystems makes the **Turbid Vision Probe**, an in-situ near-infrared sensor that measures total cell density in real time, with no sampling or dilution. It stays linear from seeding to harvest across microbial fermentation, algae cultivation and suspended-solids monitoring.

The probe integrates over I²C and has a Wi-Fi dashboard for calibration, so it suits DIY and open-source bioreactor builds.
