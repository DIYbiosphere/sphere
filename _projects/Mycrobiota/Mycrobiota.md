Mycrobiota explores humanity's relationship with the microbiota — the trillions of bacteria that inhabit the human body and outnumber our own cells — using sound as the medium of interaction.

The project draws inspiration from traditional fermentation practices like sake and miso brewing to make an invisible, symbiotic relationship perceptible.
