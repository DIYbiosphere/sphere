DIYbio Postcards was a free micro-newsletter for the DIYbio community: physical postcards mailed directly to subscribers' doors, each featuring a short list of the most interesting community work over the preceding month.

Anyone could submit an update by tweeting a short description and photo of their project tagged `#diybiohi`; entries were compiled and printed onto the next postcard run. Subscriptions were free and open to anyone via a Google Form.

<img src="https://sphere.diybio.org/projects/Postcards/postcard-mockup-front.png" class="ui image fluid small-padded" alt="Mockup of the front of a DIYbio postcard" />
<img src="https://sphere.diybio.org/projects/Postcards/postcard-mockup-back.png" class="ui image fluid small-padded" alt="Mockup of the back of a DIYbio postcard, showing the micro-update layout" />

Two volumes were mailed:

- **Vol. 1** (mailed January 31, 2012) — collected submissions from November 1, 2011 through January 1, 2012, and reached over 100 subscribers spanning six of the seven continents. Submissions included DNA extraction demos with high school students, bioluminescent algae culturing, a guide to making fluorescent yogurt, and a Makerbot-based liquid-handling hack.
- **Vol. 2** (July 2012) — open for submissions from June 12 to July 1, 2012.

<img src="https://sphere.diybio.org/projects/Postcards/vol1-subscriber-map.png" class="ui image fluid small-padded" alt="Map of Vol. 1 postcard subscribers, spanning six continents" />
<img src="https://sphere.diybio.org/projects/Postcards/vol1-postcard-in-the-wild.jpg" class="ui image fluid small-padded" alt="A Vol. 1 postcard photographed in the wild by a recipient" />

No further volumes were produced after Vol. 2. The `#diybiohi` Twitter aggregation was automated by a small companion app (`diybio-postcard-tweets`) hosted on Heroku; that app and its DNS record (`postcards.diybio.org`) have since been retired.
