Phyconauts is a community research group exploring algae science, ecology, and biomaterial potential. Members cultivate microalgae cultures, conduct DNA barcoding of local waterways, partner on urban kelp farming, and experiment with algae-derived materials.

Co-led by Sally and Greg.
