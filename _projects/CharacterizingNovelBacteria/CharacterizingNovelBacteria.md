Characterizing Novel Bacteria, led by member Mohammad Alavi, collects environmental bacterial samples, extracts and sequences their genomes, and analyzes them with tools like BLAST to identify novel metabolic pathways — open-ended exploratory work expanding the available biological toolkit, continuing from Alavi's prior work at IMET.
