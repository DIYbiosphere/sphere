AlgaeNawa makes shimenawa — traditional Shinto ritual rope, normally woven from rice straw — out of fiber from an invasive seaweed species (*Sargassum muticum*) instead.

The project pairs traditional rope-making technique with material science testing, aiming both to repurpose harmful invasive biomass for coastal ecosystem restoration and to sustain a Shinto craft practice tied to nature purification.
