Plant Cellulose from Bacteria, led by member Langston McKee, works to efficiently produce plant cellulose in bacteria using *Agrobacterium* — aiming to maximize the speed and amount of cellulose produced as a sustainable alternative to traditional forestry, with applications ranging from paper to cotton production.
