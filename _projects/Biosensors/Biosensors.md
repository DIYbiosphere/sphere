The Biosensors Community Project uses bioluminescent organisms to detect heavy metals and other environmental contaminants, working toward accessible, low-cost testing kits.

The team runs collaborative experiments on soil extraction and organism sensitivity, meeting Fridays 4–6pm at SoundBio Lab (through July 31, 2026), open to ages 12 and up.
