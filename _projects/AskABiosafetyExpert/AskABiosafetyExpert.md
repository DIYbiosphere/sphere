Ask a Biosafety Expert ("ABE") launched in January 2013 as a free educational service connecting DIY biologists with a panel of volunteer, professional biosafety experts. Anyone could submit a biosafety question online; a committee of experts would meet to discuss it and craft a response, published online for the wider community to reference alongside a growing database of previously answered questions.

The program was generously supported by a grant from the Woodrow Wilson Center for International Scholars' Synthetic Biology Project, with additional support from the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation. Its volunteer Biosafety Advisory Board over the years included university biosafety officers and Institutional Biosafety Committee members with backgrounds ranging from chemical biology to microbiology to environmental health.

ABE has since closed and is no longer accepting new questions. From its closure notice: "A huge thanks to the biosafety experts that volunteered their time over the years... If you have a biosafety question, you might find a sympathetic biosafety professional through your local university or find a local chapter through one of the professional biosafety associations."

## Further reading

For anyone doing biology outside a traditional lab setting today, these general references remain useful:

- **[American Biological Safety Association](https://www.absa.org/)** — its Biosafety Links page is a good starting point.
- **CDC's [Biosafety in Microbiological and Biomedical Laboratories](https://www.cdc.gov/labs/bmbl/index.html)** (6th Edition) — the field's standard reference, technical but thorough.
- **[NIH Office of Science Policy — Biosafety and Biosecurity Policy](https://osp.od.nih.gov/policies/biosafety-and-biosecurity-policy/)** — publishes the NIH Guidelines for Research Involving Recombinant or Synthetic Nucleic Acid Molecules; its FAQ and educational materials are more approachable than the full regulatory text.
- **[Health Canada Pathogen Safety Data Sheets](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/laboratory-biosafety-biosecurity/pathogen-safety-data-sheets-risk-assessment.html)** — safety data sheets for a wide range of infectious agents, both common and exotic.
