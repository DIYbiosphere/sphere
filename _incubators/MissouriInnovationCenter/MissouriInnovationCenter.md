The Missouri Innovation Center is a non-profit affiliate of the University of Missouri-Columbia, nurturing entrepreneurs building biotech, life-science, and deep-tech ventures in mid-Missouri since 1984. A separate organization from BioGenerator in St. Louis, though the two share some history and leadership overlap.
