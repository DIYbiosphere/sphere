KU Innovation Park is a partnership among the City of Lawrence, Douglas County, the Kansas Department of Commerce, the University of Kansas, and the Lawrence Chamber of Commerce, offering lab, office, and co-working space plus business support to bioscience and technology startups. The park system spans facilities in Lawrence and at KU Medical Center in Kansas City.
