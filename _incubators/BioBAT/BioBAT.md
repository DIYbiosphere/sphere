BioBAT is a joint venture of the NYC Economic Development Corporation and the Research Foundation for SUNY (on behalf of SUNY Downstate), offering larger-scale biotech/tech space at the Brooklyn Army Terminal for companies that have outgrown the smaller SUNY Downstate Biotechnology Incubator.
